Kyle shares her birth story

Can you imagine moving to a new country while 34 weeks pregnant?! 😱 Kyle had JUST arrived in Amsterdam days before we met at APP’s Autumn/Winter 2026-2027 Parents hang — with her belly in tow and ready to build community with local parents.

Fast forward a month or two, and her daughter has arrived. Kyle shared her birth journey with us:

  • ✈️ receiving pregnancy care in two systems (US & NL)

  • 🌪️ the many surprising twists and turns of birth

  • 🏆 the immediate relief of the walking epidural

  • 🤲🏽 and the support she felt before, during, and after.

Her post is a great read especially if you want to hear more about how walking epidurals work, and what it’s like to push (heh) through surprises and disappointments to end up with a beautiful journey anyway. Grateful to her for sharing her story — at once familiar and unique to every parent who has been through it ❤️

🗓️ What’s happening around town

Looking for something fun to do with your little one? Here's a selection of upcoming activities and events for babies, toddlers, and their parents.

The Halloween Round-up

Good for babies

  • Mama+ Club Halloween Meetup

    Mon 26 Oct @ 9:30-12:30 — De Pijp

    The weekly Mama+ Club is going full Halloween this week 🎃 Dress the little ones up, bring a small snack to share, and come enjoy the morning with fellow moms and their kids. Head to their group on WhatsApp (in the Groups Directory) for more details.

  • moveNmeet Halloween Edition (0-4 years)

    Wed 28 Oct @ 10:00-11:00 — Hoofddorppleinbuurt

    moveNmeet special alert — they're hosting a Halloween edition! Head over there that Wednesday morning for a free parent & child work-out class, and play with other families from the neighborhood before, during, and after. Costumes are optional, but 'tis the season 🦹😁 Pro tip: The café downstairs (Chef) is lovely, too.

  • Baby's First Halloween

    Thu 29 Oct @ 14:30-15:30 — De Baarsjes

    Your neighborhood witches three (myself, Sophia from Papote Cafe, and Rachael from MamaSocial) are taking over Papote's weekly Baby Hour for Halloween — spooky snacks, a Halloween book reading, and plenty of adorable, tiny costumes. A great excuse to meet other local families with babies under 1; it's part of our First Year Program, too. It's already quite packed, with just one spot remaining 🧡

  • Halloween Brunch & Costume Party

    Sat 31 Oct @ 9:30-12:00 — De Baarsjes

    It's brunch, but make it spooky 👻 Head to Papote for a festive morning with Halloween games, entertainment, and food — plus prizes for the parents and kids who arrive best dressed!

Toddler activities

  • Amsterdam Mamas Spooktacular Parade

    Sat 24 Oct @ 10:00–11:30 or 12:00–13:30 — Weteringschans

    Amsterdam Mamas’ annual Halloween parade is back at UJ Klaren Weteringpark — recommended for ages 0–8. Dress up the little ones (and the stroller!), parade through the park, and stick around for trick-or-treating, face painting, and a spooky activity from WOW Amsterdam.

  • Halloween Parade at Speeltuin Sloten

    Fri 30 Oct @ 19:00-21:00 — Sloten

    Head to Speeltuin Sloten for a festive Halloween parade and a Dracula disco. It may be a late-ish night, but it'll be a great local spot for families to gather as a community to celebrate Halloween together! (If you're a member, they have activities starting at 16:00, too.)

  • Allard Pierson Halloween Toddler Tour (2-4 years)

    Sat 31 Oct @ 10:00-11:00 — Rokin

    Alongside Paola Bucciol from Museum Beginnings, APP is hosting a museum tour of the Allard Pierson for Halloween, where we'll explore the Ancient Egypt rooms and learn all about mummies — all dressed up. The tour is sold out (🙏🏻), but we've opened up a waitlist that you're welcome to register for; we'll let you know by the 29th whether there will be space.

  • Halloween at Coffee+Kids

    Sat 31 Oct @ 10:00-16:00 — IJburg

    Coffee+Kids is getting in on the Halloween fun with spooky toddler music workshops and Halloween festivities 🍭 Book a workshop on their website or drop in for trick-or-treating at the café bar!

  • Local Trick or Treating

    Sat 31 Oct @ 16:00 — Everywhere

    Parents all across the city are organizing trick or treating for neighborhood families. I personally know of loops in West (De Baarsjes), Center (Prinseneiland), and Rivierenbuurt — the latter has been going on for years and is extremely well-done. If you live in Rivierenbuurt and you want to join in the fun, I'm happy to pass along the signup site individually. (I promised not to share in the newsletter more broadly, as the neighborhood has been overwhelmed by visitors in the past and struggled with candy supply.) If you’re trick-or-treating in someone else’s neighborhood, please consider donating candy to families as thanks for keeping the spirit alive 🎃

Parties

  • Halloween Party at Wachterliedplantsoen

    Sun 25 Oct @ 12:00-15:00 — Bos en Lommer

    A spooky afternoon for all ages at one of my favorite family hangouts in West 👻 Face painting, pumpkin decorating, spiderweb weaving, an insect corner (for the brave), and a Halloween photo op with lemonade and treats. Entry is €5 via an activity card at the KIEM kassa — or free if your kid has been earning stamps by doing chores in the park between Oct 10–24. Ask KIEM Lokaal or Wijkboerderij Wachterlied for the klusjeskaart!

  • Farmyard Halloween parties

    Sun 25 Oct — Everywhere

    A few city farms are hosting Halloween parties on Sunday, like Bijlmerweide in Zuidoost and Elsenhove in Amstelveen. The former seems to be for older kids — a spooky maze, for example — but the latter invites guests to carve pumpkins, drink witch's tea, and wander the spooky grounds. Kidsproof (linked above!) has a good list of events for "all ages" that skew towards the 3+ side.

  • Halloween at Robbeburg

    Sat 31 Oct @ 9:30-16:15 — Rivierenbuurt

    Robbeburg’s annual Halloween party runs in four time slots throughout the day (9:30, 11:15, 13:00, and 14:45). Face painting, a bouncy house, sensory play, a scavenger hunt, and crafts — plus all the usual Robbeburg fun. This one sells out every year, so book early!

  • WOW Family Center Halloween Party (3-8 years)

    Sat 31 Oct @ 14:00-15:30, 16:00-17:30 — De Pijp

    Join the WOW Family Center for a spooktacular Halloween party featuring art, fun experiments, and disco dancing 🕺🏾 The Little Café will also be open for treats and a break in between all of the fun workshops.

  • AIC Kids & Parents: The Halloween Playdate (2-10 years)

    Sat 31 Oct @ 15:00-18:00 — Houthavens

    Amsterdam International Community (AIC) are at it again! They're putting on a Halloween-themed playdate that's all out — play zone, popcorn, cotton candy, and fun activities all to celebrate the spooky season. A wonderful way to dress up in costume, let the kids run around safely, and enjoy the stunning views while connecting with local families. Plus, it's free 😎

  • Kids Halloween at de Nieuwe Anita

    Sat 31 Oct @ 15:00-18:00 — Oud-West

    If you want to find a proper American-style Halloween party, de Nieuwe Anita is throwing it. Come in costume, say “Trick or Treat” at the door for candy from the witch’s cauldron, get your face painted by the fabulous Kiki, and dance to DJ Gibbo downstairs. Cakes, a lemonade fountain, and full family chaos included — and all ages are welcome. Kids €5 at the door; adults free.

APP & Friends

  • Dutch for Parents Postpartum Post
    Starts 20 Oct — Online & In-person

    For expat parents who’ve learned Dutch (up to A2/B1) but keep defaulting to English — this is the course for you. Dutch for Parents focuses on exactly the conversations you’re actually having as a parent of a young kid in Amsterdam, during playdates, daycare pickup, and more. It includes four online meetups and (excitingly!) a free 1:1 match through Postpartum Post to practice your Dutch with another student-parent in your neighborhood. (Also excitingly!) Postpartum Post members also get 25% off the course through Post Perks, saving €83 💸

  • Spring/Summer 2027 Brunch

    Sun 1 Nov @ 12:00–13:30 — Dapperbuurt

    Due this spring or summer? Come meet other expecting moms over brunch at Bar Botanique. Organized by expecting mom Rach, this is the first in-person meetup for our Spring/Summer 2027 WhatsApp community group — whether you’re already in it or want to join.

West

  • Suzuki Music for Babies & Toddlers Fall (0-3 years)

    Thu 22 Oct @ 09:30-10:30 (Weekly) — Oud-West

    Certified Suzuki early childhood educator Hanna leads these small-group music classes for babies and toddlers plus their parents at De Hallen. Expect singing, simple percussion, movement games, storytelling, and a whole lot of joy, all rooted in Dr. Suzuki’s philosophy that every child can learn. This is the music class I bring my little one to — what I love about it is that I'm encouraged to participate as much as my child, which makes the whole session very special ❤️ Sign up for the term (until 26 Nov), or drop in for a trial class.

  • ADE babywearing dance workshop (8 weeks-18 months)

    Thu 22 Oct @ 15:30-16:30 — Westelijke Eilanden

    Feel like hitting the dance floor with your baby during the Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE)? Annick — the powerhouse behind GroovaRoo — is hosting a babywearing dance workshop for moms and dads with babies up to 18 months. No dance experience required, as the motto is "connection over perfection."

  • Contakids: Parent & child movement class (22 months-6 years)

    Starting 23 Oct (Weekly) — Oud West

    "The Contakids class is an hour of BEING together." The focus is using contact and movement to deepen the parent-child connection while kids build motor skills and confidence and parents practice the art of trusting their little one (and themselves). The new 7-week series starts this week, and there are still a few spots left.

  • Carry & Groove 6-week course (0-2 years)

    Wed starting 28 Oct @ 13:00-14:00 & Friday starting 30 Oct @ 11:00-12:00 (Weekly) — Oud-West

    Join Maria from Carry & Groove for the second round of her 6-week babywearing dance course. It's designed around real choreography (she's a professional dancer!) set to afrobeats and dancehall — as well as around building a community for like-minded moms week after week.

South

  • Mom Meetup at Monti's World

    Fri 23 Oct @ 10:30-12:00 — De Pijp

    Charlotte from de kleine village is hosting a playdate at Monti's World to bring moms together while the kids explore. The playroom is set up so little ones can discover and climb at their own pace, so the moms get a moment to sit down and connect with each other.

  • Who Am I As A Parent: Understanding Your Parenting Approach

    Fri 30 Oct @ 18:30-19:30 — De Pijp

    Family counsellor Ana Rodrigues is leading a workshop on understanding your relationship with your child, your parenting approach and the kind of parent you want to be. You'll explore concepts like authority without authoritarianism, boundaries that don’t break connection, and what to do when you’re not sure if you handled it right. Practical, reflective, and judgment-free ❤️

  • Language Parade: Language, Music, and Fun (0-12 years)

    Sat 7 Nov @ 11:00-16:00 — Amstelveen

    Our local multilingual professional legend, Mimi (from Growing Up Multilingual), is heading up the Language Parade, a multilingual celebration of language, music, and culture at Bibliotheek Amstelveen. Some of the events include stories read aloud in English, Spanish, Chinese, Turkish, and Arabic; a kids’ disco and silent disco; sock puppet crafting; an Indonesian live band; and a sign language workshop. Whatever language you speak at home, you’re welcome. Free, no registration needed, drop in anytime ❤️
