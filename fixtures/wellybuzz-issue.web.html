🐝 Halloween Weekend: Boo at the Zoo, Witches Market & the Big Top

Plus Creepy Cute Market, a Karori haunted house, an Opera House clue hunt, Louis Baker live + the Kāpiti Women's Expo turns 10. Plus WIN Well Ahead passes! 🎃

Together with
Kāpiti Women's Expo - Sun 1 November
👋 Welcome

Kia ora Wellington! 🌸

Halloween weekend is nearly here and Wellington's going all out! The Zoo throws its first-ever Boo at the Zoo, the Royal New Zealand Ballet's Sleeping Beauty takes the St James stage, Weber Bros pitch their big top in Upper Hutt, Wellington Repertory Theatre's thriller Accomplice plays the Gryphon, and the Kāpiti Women's Expo celebrates 10 years on Sunday, when the Witches Market also casts its spell over Brewtown. Saturday's morning rain should clear early, so dust off those costumes. Let's get buzzing! 🐝

🎁 WIN! 5 double passes to Well AheadWe've teamed up with Conquer Health to give away 5 double passes to Well Ahead (worth $220 each, as full passes are $110) - a day built around women's health, resilience and healthy ageing, with speakers including Dame Susan Devoy, at Victoria University Kelburn on Thursday 12 November. To enter, just reply to this email with the words WELL AHEAD by 5pm Wednesday 4 November.Find out more about Well Ahead

🎉 Celebrate 10 Years of the Kāpiti Women’s Expo 🎉

Sunday 1 November 2026 | 10am–3pm
Southward Car Museum
Gold Coin Entry
Proceeds proudly supporting Kāpiti Women’s Refuge

Come along and help us celebrate the 10th Birthday of the Kāpiti Women’s Expo under the direction of Julie and Emily.

With over 100 sites under one roof, there really is something for everyone. Discover a fantastic mix of art, beauty, fashion, food and drink, health and fitness, homeware, pet care, services, travel and tourism, and so much more.

It’s the perfect opportunity to get a head start on your Christmas shopping while supporting New Zealand-based businesses, all in one convenient location. Bring the whole family, enjoy a fun day out, discover something new and support a great local cause.

With so much to see and do, this is a 10th Birthday celebration not to be missed!

For a full list of exhibitors, visit www.KapitiWomensExpo.co.nz

And while you're there, why not make a day of it and enjoy a visit to the Southward Car Museum too.

We look forward to celebrating 10 amazing years with you
