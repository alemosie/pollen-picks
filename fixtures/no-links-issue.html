The 4 pm wall

Somewhere between nap and dinner, the day tips over. Everyone is tired, nobody is hungry for what is on the table, and the smallest thing (the wrong cup, a sock seam) is a catastrophe. Several of you wrote in about it this month, so here is what the group has been trying:

  • 🥕 a snack plate on the floor at 3:45, before anyone asks

  • 🚿 a warm bath earlier, instead of after dinner

  • 🎧 a podcast for the grown-up in the room, while the kid plays alone for ten minutes

🗓️ Nothing on the calendar

A small thing to try this week

Pick one evening, turn the overhead lights off, and use lamps only. Several parents say bedtime gets calmer within three nights. Tell us if it works for you, reply to this email and we will share the best stories next time.
